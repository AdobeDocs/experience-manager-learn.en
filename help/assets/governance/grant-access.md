# Grant access to AEM Assets {#grant-access}

Access to AEM Assets starts in the [Adobe Admin Console](https://adminconsole.adobe.com), where product profiles control what users can do in your AEM as a Cloud Service author environment. In this video, you learn how to:

* Find your AEM as a Cloud Service author environment and its product profiles in the Admin Console.
* Choose between the three AEM Assets product profiles: **AEM Assets Collaborator Users**, **AEM Assets Power Users**, and **AEM Administrators**.
* Create a user group and assign it a product profile, so you manage access by group instead of one user at a time.
* Add users to a group as your team grows or changes.

>[!VIDEO](https://video.tv.adobe.com/v/3504447?quality=12&learn=on)

For more on managing products and user access, see [Managing products and user access in the Admin Console](https://experienceleague.adobe.com/docs/experience-manager-cloud-service/security/ims-support.html#managing-products-and-user-access-in-admin-console).

After users have access to your environment, control which folders and content they can work with in [Permissions management](./permissions-management.md).
