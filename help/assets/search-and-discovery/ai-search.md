# AI search {#ai-search}

AI Search understands the meaning behind a query, not just its exact keywords, so a search can succeed even when the asset's metadata uses different words entirely.

>[!BEGINTABS]

>[!TAB Assets view]

## Enable AI search

Turn on AI Search from General Settings > Search.

>[!VIDEO](https://video.tv.adobe.com/v/3479989/?learn=on&enablevpops)

>[!TAB Admin view]

## Enable AI search

Turn on AI Search from Tools > Assets > Assets Configurations > Assets OmniSearch Configuration. The Assets view and Admin view settings are independent, so you can enable AI Search in either or both.

>[!VIDEO](https://video.tv.adobe.com/v/3479989/?learn=on&enablevpops)

>[!ENDTABS]
