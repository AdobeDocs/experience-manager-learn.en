# Governance Agent

The Governance Agent helps protect brand integrity and ensure compliance across Adobe Experience Manager by enforcing security, regulatory, and brand policies on all content interactions and activations. Key capabilities include automated brand governance to maintain consistency and permissions/DRM controls to ensure secure, compliant use of digital assets.
