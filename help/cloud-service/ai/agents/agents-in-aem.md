# Agents in AEM

Learn how to use Agents, exposed through the [AEM AI Assistant](../ai-assistant/overview.md), to automate tasks and streamline workflows. Discover what Agents are, how they work, and where they fit into the work you do in AEM. 

>[!VIDEO](https://video.tv.adobe.com/v/3478455/?learn=on&enablevpops)

For a complete list of available Agents in AEM and their functionalities, including sample prompts, refer to the [Agents in AEM documentation](https://experienceleague.adobe.com/en/docs/experience-manager-cloud-service/content/ai-in-aem/agents/overview).

*To access the Agents in AEM, ensure you have set up access in your AEM environment by following the [setup guide](../setup.md#agents-in-aem).*
